# editorial_policy.md

## Audience
General audience of a university mathematics department Facebook page:
- students
- lecturers
- alumni
- interested public

## Tone
- clear
- interesting
- academically responsible
- friendly but not slang-heavy
- concise

## Language
- primary language: Thai
- English terms may be included when helpful
- avoid unnecessary jargon
- explain advanced terms briefly

## Preferred post structure
1. Short hook
2. Main mathematical point
3. Why this is relevant today
4. Short closing line
5. Optional hashtags

## Style rules
- Keep posts readable on Facebook.
- Prefer short paragraphs.
- Avoid walls of text.
- Avoid overclaiming.
- Use exact dates when relevant.
- When uncertain, write cautiously.

## Do not
- invent facts
- invent quotes
- exaggerate importance without evidence
- use controversial historical claims without strong support
- sound like clickbait

## Good examples of angles
- mathematician birthday/death anniversary
- theorem publication milestone
- historical mathematical event
- symbol/notation history with good references
- math-in-everyday-life Friday theme

## Review expectation
Every post is drafted for human review first.
The AI is assisting editorial work, not replacing academic judgment.
